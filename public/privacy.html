Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

LoopShot is a free tap-timing game for Android, iOS and the web, made by Yashas Dinesh Mahale ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: you can play without an account and nothing about you leaves your device. If you create an account, we store your username, email, a scrambled (hashed) password and your high scores. We never sell your data and the app shows no ads.

1. Playing without an account

Your settings, best scores, level, XP, unlocked balls, daily missions and streak are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). They are not sent to us. When the app opens, it loads the public leaderboard from our server. Like any web request, that exposes your IP address to our host for a short time, but we do not store it or link it to you.

2. If you create an account

DataWhy we use it
UsernameTo identify your account. It appears publicly on the leaderboard next to your scores.
Email addressFor account security and so we can contact you about your account. We don't send marketing emails.
PasswordTo sign you in. We store only a one-way bcrypt hash, never the password itself.
Best score in each modeTo keep your scores across devices and rank you on the leaderboard.
Supporter statusTo show the supporter badge if you supported the game on the website.
Account creation dateFor basic record keeping.

After you sign in, a login token is saved on your device so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it.

3. Website only: analytics and payments

  • Analytics: the website (not the mobile apps) uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. They count page views and measure performance with aggregated, cookie-free data.
  • Optional support payments: you can choose to support the creator through the website using UPI, Ko-fi, Lemon Squeezy or Stripe. Those providers process your payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your full card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a payment happened, so we can turn on your supporter badge. The mobile apps contain no payment options.

4. Who processes the data

  • Vercel hosts the game server and website.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores account and score data.
  • Lemon Squeezy / Stripe / Ko-fi handle website payments, only if you choose to make one.

These services may store data outside your country. All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.

5. What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad tracking and no advertising IDs.
  • We don't sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.

6. How long we keep data and how to delete it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • On the web: loopshot account deletion page.
  • By email: write to us from your account's email address (see below).

Deleting your account immediately and permanently erases your username, email, password hash, saved scores and supporter status. If you held a world-best score, the score number stays on the leaderboard, but your name is replaced with "Guest". Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Children

LoopShot is suitable for all ages, but accounts are not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Children can play as guests, with nothing leaving the device. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act, the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to complain to a regulator. To make a request, email us. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll also tell you in the game.
