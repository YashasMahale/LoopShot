How to play

  1. The ball loops around the ring. Watch for the dark arc.
  2. Tap anywhere (or press Space) while the ball is inside the arc. Each hit flips its direction.
  3. Land on the white dot for a perfect, worth 2 points. Three perfects in a row and you’re on fire.
  4. Every run earns XP. Missions and a daily streak earn more, and new levels unlock new balls.
