Delete your account

This page lets you permanently delete your LoopShot account (by Yashas Dinesh Mahale), even if you no longer have the app installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your username, email address and password hash
  • Your saved best scores in every mode
  • Your supporter status

Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you held a world-best score, the score number stays on the leaderboard, but your name is replaced with "Guest". Nothing else is kept.

Option 1: in the app

Open LoopShot → Settings → Account → Delete Account, then confirm with your password.

Option 2: right here

Option 3: by email

Forgot your password? Email yashasmahale@gmail.com from the address on your account and include your username. We'll delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.
